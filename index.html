Common questions
Can I buy a tee on this site?

No. This site is a showcase of GOOBA designs and has no shop or checkout. If a design catches your eye, use the Enquire button on its page.

How do I ask about a specific design?

Open the design and select Enquire. The form fills in the design name for you.

Where do new designs appear first?

On the Tees page, marked New for their first month, and in the feed on the home page.
